About the Calm & Oak Home Collection

The Calm & Oak Home Collection is Japandi home decor drawn in our own studio — twelve original artworks in textured mineral paint, torn washi and kintsugi gold, made to order as art prints, stoneware mugs, cotton totes and instant digital downloads. Every piece is printed on demand and shipped from the facility nearest you, so nothing is warehoused and nothing is wasted. If you’re building a whole room, start with our shoppable Looks, learn the principles in the Journal, or begin with the free guide on our Begin Here page.
