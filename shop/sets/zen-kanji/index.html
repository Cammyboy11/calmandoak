Zen Kanji Set

Three words. Three moments of stillness.

The story

This matched series brings together Ma (間), Wa (和), and Shizuka (静) — three Japanese concepts that speak not through decoration, but through restraint. Together they create a quiet rhythm across the wall: space, harmony, and stillness.

Each character is brushed with deliberate simplicity against a wide field of open paper, signed with its English caption and finished with a vermillion seal. Hung together, the prints feel less like artwork and more like an atmosphere — inviting a room to slow down without asking for attention.

They belong where simplicity has room to breathe — above a sofa, along a hallway, behind a dining table, or wherever a wall asks for calm rather than clutter.
