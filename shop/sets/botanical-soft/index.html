Botanical Soft Set

Three stems. Three expressions of quiet.

The story

The Botanical Soft Set brings together three delicate botanical studies — Single Stem, Olive, and Eucalyptus. Each celebrates the softer side of nature through slender forms, muted tones, and generous space.

Single Stem is the simplest of the three — one delicate stem standing quietly on its own. Olive brings a little more structure, with its small leaves and gentle rhythm. Eucalyptus introduces softness and movement through its rounded leaves and airy branches.

The palette remains soft and restrained, allowing each botanical to breathe. They belong above a bed, beside a reading chair, along a hallway, or anywhere you want to bring a softer, more organic feeling into the room.
