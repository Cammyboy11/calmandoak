Calm Waters Set

Three studies in movement, stillness, and everything in between.

The story

The Calm Waters set brings together three original sumi-e studies — Koi, Mountain Stream, and Chawan — connected by the quiet language of water. Koi moves beneath the surface. Mountain Stream follows its path. Chawan holds the stillness.

Together, they create a quiet visual rhythm inspired by traditional Japanese ink painting, where a few deliberate marks and generous open space can say more than a crowded composition ever could.

Designed to hang as a triptych or individually throughout the home, the three works share the same restrained palette and atmosphere — creating a cohesive gallery wall without feeling overly matched.

It belongs wherever a room could use a little more calm — above a sofa, along a hallway, beside a reading chair, or across three walls that could use a quiet connection.
