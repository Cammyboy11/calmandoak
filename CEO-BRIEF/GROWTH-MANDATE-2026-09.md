# Growth Mandate — 2026-09-14

Cameron's own framing, verbatim in spirit: *"We are building a global powerhouse brand... this is
the only way one man can own and operate a multi-million dollar business. The roots and bones are
there, now it is up to you to take my dream to a reality."* Every function below needs: **a defined
success metric, and a standing commitment to rigorously improve both quality and quantity of it
over time** — not a one-time build.

## 1. Social media — platform-native, not one-size-fits-all
High-quality content across **Pinterest, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube** — each piece *designed for
that specific platform*, not the same asset reposted four ways. Grounded in sound research (what
actually works per platform, not assumption). Use the best tools available within budget.

## 2. Merchandise — zero inventory, research-driven, expanding categories
Agents building real product in the POD/dropship providers (**Gelato, Printful**, etc.) based on
market research, linked into the store. Explicitly a **zero-inventory model** — this is not
incidental, it's a constraint on every product decision. Current state: `calmoak-merchandiser` is
building t-shirts. Cameron wants **more apparel, home goods, mugs, ceramics** — "great designs
based on research," not just more of the same SKU shape.

## 3. Product distribution — more eyeballs, more places
Agents creating product photography/imagery, then **posting across external marketplaces** beyond
calmandoak.com — **Etsy, Payhip, Google Merchant Center** named explicitly. The stated goal: "the
more eyeballs we can get on a growing number of products the better." This is a distribution
function, distinct from #2 (which builds the product) and #1 (which drives traffic via content).

## 4. Shopify store — near-complete, wants help, specifics not yet given
Cameron is "almost done" building a Shopify store — art is live on it now, wants more listed. He
said he "could use a lot of help with" it but didn't specify what kind (content population, product
uploads, design polish, technical/app setup, launch checklist). **Needs a clarifying question before
any agent acts on this** — don't guess at scope for a storefront that's this close to launch.

## 5. Commercial reporting — live, consolidated
Agents reporting **live-time sales and key metrics** to the CEO — including who's visiting the
site. Explicitly named sources to consolidate: **Google Search Console, Amazon Associates**, plus
whatever else actually holds the numbers (Payhip, Etsy, Shopify once live, Blotato). This is the
measurement-gap problem (GA4/GSC/Awin/MailerLite all disconnected, flagged independently at least
five times since June) elevated to a first-class, named function — not just "someone should wire
a credential eventually."

## 6. Partnerships division
People (agents) running partnerships — not a side task bolted onto outreach. Matches the existing
"Brand & Influencer Partnerships" gap in `ORG-CHART.md`'s Planned section; Cameron is now naming it
directly as a priority, not something to defer indefinitely.

## 7. Market opportunity scouting — continuous, not periodic
People "always looking for new commercial opportunities." Matches the existing "Market Research &
Commercialization Scout" gap in `ORG-CHART.md`. Cameron's framing ("always") argues against the
CEO's earlier biweekly-cadence recommendation — worth the CEO revisiting that call against this
mandate rather than assuming the old cadence still fits.

## 8. Quality — Picks & Looks
The Picks/Looks pages on the site exist; Cameron wants their **quality** improved. Not a build gap
— an existing function (content-factory/merchandiser territory) that needs a raised bar, with a
defined standard for what "better" means.

## 9. Quality — the Magazine (journal), and auto-publishing
The journal ("magazine") on the site needs improved quality **and** to become **auto-published** —
i.e., the content pipeline that writes and ships new journal articles should run without a human
click once an article clears its quality gate. This is the seo-ranker/content-writer function,
now with an explicit auto-publish requirement layered on top of the just-added review-gate policy
(CEO review still applies — "auto-published" means no *Cameron* click, not no review at all).

## The standing instruction, not a one-time task
> "All of these details need to be meticulously covered by you and you need to define what success
> is for each and to rigorously improve the quality and quantity of each."

This is not "build these once." It's a standing mandate: every function above needs (a) a defined,
written success metric, (b) a real owner, and (c) evidence over time that both quality and quantity
are actually improving — the same evidence-based discipline as the execution-integrity check, applied
to growth instead of just uptime. The CEO's monthly strategic review (`CEO-OPERATING-BRIEF.md`) is
where this gets tracked going forward — this document is the input, not the tracking mechanism
itself.
